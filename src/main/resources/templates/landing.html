How It Works

Simple Steps to Rescue Food

Our platform makes it effortless for restaurants to donate surplus food and for NGOs to collect it — all in real time.

Step 01

Restaurants List Surplus

Restaurants add details about surplus food — type, quantity, expiry time, and pickup location — in just a few clicks.

Step 02

NGOs Accept Donations

NGOs browse available donations near them, accept the ones they can handle, and coordinate pickups seamlessly.

Step 03

Track & Deliver

Track donation status in real time via our interactive map. Food gets delivered to those who need it most.

Who Is FoodRescue For?

Whether you run a restaurant, lead an NGO, or manage city programs — FoodRescue has the tools for you.

Restaurants & Cafés

Turn surplus food into community impact. List donations in seconds, track your contribution history, and build your brand as a responsible business.

NGOs & Charities

Find surplus food near you instantly. Accept donations, manage pickups, and view restaurant locations on an interactive map.

City Administrators

Monitor food rescue operations across your city. Access reports, manage users, and track food waste reduction metrics from a central dashboard.
